# Forkward

> A wandering eater's almanack for choosing what to eat, abroad and otherwise.

Forkward is for the moment when everyone is hungry and nobody wants to make the call. Set a location, choose how far you are willing to go, narrow the mood by cuisine or price, and get a focused shortlist of nearby food spots.

When indecision wins, hit **Surprise Me** and let Forkward pick.

## What It Does

- Finds nearby places to eat from a location or GPS
- Filters by distance, cuisine, and price
- Shows results in a compact shortlist
- Includes a map view for nearby options
- Saves favourites on your device
- Picks a random option when you cannot decide

## Why Forkward

Forkward is not trying to be an exhaustive restaurant directory. It is built to make choosing easier: fewer tabs, fewer endless lists, and a little more momentum toward actually eating.
